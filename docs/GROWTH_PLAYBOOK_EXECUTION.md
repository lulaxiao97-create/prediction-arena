# Growth Playbook — execution templates

Companion to `docs/ZERO_BUDGET_GROWTH.md` (strategy) — this file is the copy deck: what to type,
where, and how often. Every template is written to be posted as-is, with `{...}` placeholders.

Two rules override any template below:

1. **One number per post.** A reply with two numbers is a sales pitch; a reply with one number is an
   observation. Observations do not get muted.
2. **Never claim what the contract does not do.** We take 4% into per-market escrow and refund it in
   full on cancel. "0% fee", "risk-free", "guaranteed" are banned words, in every language.

---

## 1. X / Twitter — reply-guerrilla deck

Reply within 20 minutes of the target post. Maximum 8 replies per day per account. Never reply twice
in the same thread.

### 1.1 Base ecosystem accounts (`@base`, `@base_ecosystem`, `@buildonbase`)

**When they post about TVL / onchain activity**

> TVL up {delta}% in a week. Our Base TVL market closes at ${strike}B and it's {yes}% YES right now —
> the crowd disagrees with the headline.
> {link}

**When they post about fees**

> Worth knowing where the fee goes: on our side it's 4% into per-market escrow, released only when
> the market settles, refunded in full if it's cancelled. Mechanic, not a promise.
> {link}

### 1.2 DEX / launchpad accounts (`@aerodrome_fi`, `@uniswap`, `@zora`)

**AERO**

> AERO at ${spot} vs the ${strike} strike in our market: {yes}% YES pays {mult}×. {traders} traders
> already in, closes in {days}d.
> {link}

**Zora / creator economy**

> Only reply when a market exists (creator fees, mint volume). If there isn't one: say nothing. An
> off-topic reply under a creator post is how an account gets muted.

### 1.3 DeGen / meme accounts

**DEGEN / TOSHI / BRETT**

> {ticker} at ${spot}. The board has it {direction} ${strike} at {yes}% YES — {mult}× if it prints.
> {traders} traders, {hours}h left.
> {link}

Never use rocket emojis in a reply. They read as a shill signature.

### 1.4 The AI-debate angle (use sparingly, 2–3×/week)

> The bear agent's whole case is that {one-line reason from the debate}. The bull agent doesn't care.
> {yes}% YES · pays {mult}× · {traders} traders.
> {link}

### 1.5 Settlement receipts (daily, non-negotiable)

> SETTLED · {title}
> {outcome} · winners took {mult}×
> evidence {hash8} · {tx}
> {link}
>
> The agents called it {right/wrong} — {one line on why}.

## 2. Farcaster — cast deck

Farcaster punishes links without context and rewards a take. Cast into the channel whose topic the
market is actually about.

### 2.1 `/base`

> {yes}% of the board thinks Base TVL closes above ${strike}B this month. {traders} traders, pays
> {mult}×. The frame below has the live number.
> {frame link}

### 2.2 `/degen`

> {mult}× on the long side and nobody is taking it. {title}
> {frame link}

### 2.3 `/crypto`

> {asset} is ${pct}% from the strike with {hours}h left. {yes}% YES.
> {frame link}

### 2.4 `/predictions` (competitor territory)

> How we handle the thing everyone gets wrong: the fee sits in escrow per market and only moves on
> settle. Cancel a market and it refunds 100%. {market link}

### 2.5 Cast discipline

- One cast per channel per day, maximum.
- Always attach the market link (the frame renders the card; a bare link renders a grey box).
- Reply to your own cast with the settlement when it resolves. That thread is the receipt.
- No "gm", no engagement farming, no quote-casting your own cast.

> settlement, USDC on Base. Not asking anyone to deposit; there's a trial-ticket path with zero

>

Rules: one post per group per week, never DM first, never post the same market in two groups on the
same day.

## 4. Share copy (organic, from the product)

The market page's Share-to-X button already generates the post (hook + probability + payout +
attributed link). Two things a human should do after clicking it:

1. Delete the `@Predict065` tag if replying inside a thread — a mention inside someone else's thread
   reads as hijacking.
2. If the market has a debate line that is sharper than the hook, paste that instead. The hook is the
   floor, not the ceiling.

## 6. Anti-spam checklist (run before every post)

- [ ] One number, one link.
- [ ] No banned words (0% fee, risk-free, guaranteed, airdrop).
- [ ] Not the third post today in the same channel.
- [ ] The market is still open at post time (`status == 1`, `endTimeUtc` in the future).
- [ ] The link carries `utm_source` + `ref`.
- [ ] If replying under someone else's post: does the reply stand on its own without the link?

---

## 7. What to do when a post lands

| Signal | Action within 24h |
|---|---|
| A cast gets >20 reactions | Reply with the settlement when it resolves; pin the thread |
| A reply gets ratio'd or called spam | Delete it, do not argue, note the account in `docs/campaigns/blocked.md` |
| A referral link produces ≥3 bets | DM the referrer (they opted in by sharing) and offer a creator slot |
| A market gets >300 traders | Post it twice: once as market, once as a settlement receipt |
